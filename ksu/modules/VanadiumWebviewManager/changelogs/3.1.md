Vanadium is the privacy and security focused browser and Android System WebView from GrapheneOS, based on Chromium. It includes hardening against exploits, reduced attack surface and privacy improvements, and it receives frequent updates to stay in sync with upstream Chromium security fixes.

This module installs and updates Vanadium WebView, Browser and Config, and sets Vanadium as the active system WebView.
